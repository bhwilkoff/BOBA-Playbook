## BASELINE — established 2026-06-19

### Set Overview

- **Full name**: Bo Jackson Battle Arena: Tecmo Bowl Edition
- **Theme**: Celebrates 1987 Tecmo Bowl video game (Bo Jackson's most-noted game appearance)
- **Release date**: June 18, 2026 (pushed from Feb 2026 → May 1 → June 18)
- **Delay cause**: Factory production delay, announced on Whatnot stream with Bo and Barry
- **Framework**: Completely redesigned for the Tecmo Bowl collaboration

### Confirmed Card Types

- **Base set**: Full hero set with the standard BOBA treatment/weapon structure
- **Inspired Ink Autographs**: ON-CARD ONLY — no sticker autos. Found in both SKUs.
- **SP Chase Inserts**: 8-bit alt-art cards (FIRST EVER in Battle Arena)
- **SSP Chase Inserts**: 8-bit alt-art cards (FIRST EVER in Battle Arena)
- **Black Variation Touchdown Bo Jackson**: /34 — gold ink signature
- **Touchdown Bo Jackson 1/1**: On-card autograph, 1-of-1
