BOBA PLAYBOOK

BOBA Playbook has shut down.

November 21, 2026

BOBA Playbook was an unofficial, fan-built companion app for the Bo Jackson Battle Arena trading card game. It let you search cards, scan them, track your collection, build decks and look up rules.

We never received approval from the game's publisher to operate it, so we've shut it down. The web app and the Android app are no longer available, and the iOS beta has ended.

Thank you to everyone who tested it, filed bugs, and shared their binders with us.

Your data

All accounts, collections, decks and avatars will be permanently deleted by November 28, 2026. You don't need to do anything. If you want your data deleted sooner, or a copy of your collection first, email privacy@bobaplaybook.com. More about data deletion.

For builders

The code is open source under the MIT license. The post mortem covers what we built, what went wrong, and which pieces are worth reusing. Card content belongs to its owners and isn't included. If you want to build in this space, talk to the publisher first.
